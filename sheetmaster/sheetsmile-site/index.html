Your form shouldn't need a server

A simple contact form on your website secretly demands a server, a database, spam protection, email notifications, and a deployment pipeline — before you collect a single submission.

Google Forms takes visitors off your site. Building a backend yourself takes days. SheetSmile gives you one URL — point any HTML form at it, and every submission becomes a row in your own Google Sheet. No server. No database. No code.

Connect, create, collect — in two minutes

  • Connect your Google account

    Sign in with Google in one click. Paste a sheet URL or let us create one in your Drive — your data stays in your account, access revocable anytime.

  • Create your form

    Change your existing form's action URL, build one in the drag-and-drop builder, paste the iframe embed, or copy the generated HTML — four ways, one endpoint.

  • See rows appear in your sheet

    Every submission becomes a new row in seconds — columns auto-created and auto-mapped, timestamped, with email and Slack notifications. The one-line integration is the demo.

Four ways to connect, endless features

Build & share your way

Use the drag-and-drop builder with 11 field types and live preview. Or change your existing form's action URL, paste an iframe embed, copy the generated HTML, or share via QR code — pick what fits your workflow.

Submissions appear as rows in seconds with auto-mapped columns and timestamps. File uploads go straight to your Drive. Get email and Slack notifications, connect webhooks to Zapier, and set submission caps with public spots-taken bars — all with zero backend code.
