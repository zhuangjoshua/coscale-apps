Questions you probably have

How does SheetSmile work?

You sign in with your Google account, create a form (paste an existing sheet URL or let us create one in your Drive), and get a unique endpoint URL. Point any website form at that URL and every submission appears as a new row in your spreadsheet within seconds.

Do my website visitors need a Google account?

No. Your visitors never see Google at all. They fill out your form on your site like any normal form. SheetSmile handles the rest behind the scenes.

How do I connect my existing form to SheetSmile?

Copy your unique endpoint URL from the SheetSmile dashboard and paste it into your form's action attribute. Live in under a minute. You can also use our drag-and-drop builder, embed an iframe, paste generated HTML, or share a QR code.

Is my data secure? Where does it live?

Your spreadsheet stays in your own Google Drive. SheetSmile writes submissions to it but never copies or stores your data elsewhere. You can revoke access from your Google account settings at any time.

Can I get notified when someone submits a form?

Yes. Turn on email notifications, Slack messages, or Discord alerts. Each notification includes the submission details and a link to your sheet.

What happens when I reach my free plan limit?

The free plan includes up to two forms and about 100 submissions per month. When you hit that limit, new submissions are paused until the next month. Upgrade to Pro to remove all limits.

How do I manage my subscription?

Sign in to SheetSmile with Google and open your account profile. From there you can upgrade, change your plan, update billing, or cancel anytime.

Can I use SheetSmile with my static site or site builder?

Yes. SheetSmile works with any HTML form on any platform: static sites, Webflow, Squarespace, Carrd, WordPress, hand-coded pages, and more. If the platform lets you edit a form action URL or paste an embed, it works.

What file types can visitors upload?

Visitors can upload images, PDFs, documents, and more through your form. The files land directly in your Google Drive. No visitor login needed.

Do you offer autoresponders or custom email replies?

Automatic reply emails are coming soon. In the meantime, use our open webhooks to trigger your own email workflow through Zapier or any custom integration.

Ready to stop building backends for simple forms?

Connect your Google account, create a form, and see submissions appear as rows in your sheet — all in under two minutes.
