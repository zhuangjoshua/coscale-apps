# Pagewright

**Business name:** Pagewright

**Business description:** Pagewright is a document generation platform that turns a business's data into finished, on-brand paperwork automatically. Teams design a document once — a quote, invoice, report, certificate or contract — mark which parts change, and then produce unlimited personalised versions from a form, a spreadsheet, or their own software, without anyone reformatting a template by hand.

**Target customer:** Contractors and trade businesses, professional-services firms, agencies, training providers, property and field-service companies, and software teams whose product needs to send documents to customers.

**Core value proposition:** Eliminate the manual work of assembling customer paperwork. Every quote, invoice and report comes out correctly formatted, correctly priced, and consistently branded — produced in seconds instead of half an hour in a word processor.

**How it works:** A business builds its document in a visual editor from ready-made sections — headers, customer details, line-item tables, totals, terms and signature blocks — and marks which parts are filled from data. From then on, the document is generated automatically: staff enter the details, a spreadsheet is uploaded, or the business's own software sends the data, and Pagewright returns the finished file ready to send. Documents grow and shrink to fit their contents, so a quote with four line items and one with two hundred both come out correct.

**Core capabilities:** Visual document editor, reusable templates, line-item tables that flow across pages with repeating column headers, running headers and footers with automatic page numbers, sections that appear or disappear based on the data, totals and taxes calculated from the line items, custom branding and themes, fillable fields customers can sign on screen, PDF and image output, document merging, live preview, a generation API with keys and webhooks, and a history of every document produced.

**AI capabilities:** *(Not yet built — listed as intended scope, not current functionality.)* Drafting a template from a plain-English description, writing scope-of-work and terms copy from a short summary, extracting line items from a photo or an existing document, and flagging quotes that are priced inconsistently with previous jobs.

**Typical use cases:** Contractor quotes and estimates, invoices and statements, client-facing performance reports, course and training certificates, inspection and site reports, service agreements, delivery notes and packing slips, and any document a software product needs to email to its own customers.

**Differentiators:** Most tools in this space are image generators that treat a document as a fixed-size canvas, so anything of unpredictable length has to be stitched together page by page. Pagewright is built for documents that change size: tables break across pages properly, headers repeat, totals are calculated rather than typed in, and sections vanish when they do not apply. It is also usable by the person who owns the document — an office manager or business owner — rather than requiring a developer, while still exposing a clean API for teams that want to automate it.

**Brand positioning:** Precise, dependable, unfussy. Paperwork that comes out right every time.

**Primary conversion goal:** Get a business to build or customise their first template and generate a real document they would actually send to a customer.
